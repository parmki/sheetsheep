Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Applies to: the SheetSheep mobile application and this website

SheetSheep is a sight-reading practice tool. It needs two things from you — a microphone to hear your piano, and optionally a Google account to sync your progress. This policy states plainly what happens to each. The short version: audio never leaves your device, and we never sell your data.

The two things people ask about most:

1. Microphone. Audio is analysed live, on your device, to detect which note you played. It is never recorded, never saved, and never transmitted to us or anyone else.

2. Google Sign-In. We receive your email address, name and profile identifier for one reason: to create your profile and sync lesson progress across your devices. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it for advertising.

1. Who we are

SheetSheep ("we", "us", "the app") is an independently developed mobile application. For any privacy question, request, or complaint, contact: sheetsheepsupport@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information from Google Sign-In (optional)

You can use SheetSheep's curriculum without an account. If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive the standard profile information that Google releases to an app using the email, profile and openid scopes:

  • your email address,
  • your display name,
  • your Google account identifier (a stable numeric ID),
  • your profile picture URL, if your Google account has one.

How we use it: solely to create and identify your SheetSheep profile and to synchronise your lesson progress, streaks and drill statistics across the devices you sign in on. That is the entire purpose.

What we never do with it: we never sell, rent, trade or otherwise disclose your Google account data to third parties for advertising, marketing, profiling or any other purpose. We do not use it to build advertising profiles. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts or any other Google service — the scopes we request do not permit it.

Google's own handling of your account is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke SheetSheep's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

2.2 Microphone and audio data

Microphone audio is processed entirely in real time, on your device, for pitch detection — and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted to external servers.

The app requests microphone permission for one feature: detecting the acoustic pitch of the note you play on a real piano or keyboard during drills. Audio is captured and analysed locally, on the device, inside the app's audio processing path. Pitch results are used to score the drill in the moment. The raw audio stream is discarded continuously as it is processed.

No audio recording is created. No audio file is written to your device's storage. No audio is uploaded, streamed, or otherwise sent to our servers or to any third party. There is no server-side audio processing of any kind.

You can decline or revoke microphone permission at any time in your device settings. The app remains fully usable without it — you simply lose live acoustic pitch detection and can continue with touch input.

2.3 Practice data stored on your device

SheetSheep is local-first. Your drill results, completed lessons, practice streaks, accuracy statistics and app preferences are stored on your device using local device storage (AsyncStorage). This data does not leave your device unless you sign in with Google, in which case it is synchronised to your own profile so your other devices can pick up where you left off.

2.4 Purchase information

SheetSheep Pro is sold through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Payments are processed entirely by those stores — we never see or store your card number, billing address, or any payment credentials. We receive only a confirmation of entitlement (whether a Pro purchase is active) so the app can unlock the paid features.

2.5 What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your photos, your device calendar, your health data, or your advertising identifier for tracking purposes. We do not run third-party behavioural tracking or sell audience data.

3. How information is shared

We share data only in these narrow cases:

Recipient What and why
Google (Sign-In) Provides your authentication and profile identity, only if you choose to sign in.
Apple App Store / Google Play Process your Pro purchase and report entitlement status back to the app.
Hosting & backend providers Store the synced profile and progress records described in section 2.3, on our behalf.
Legal obligation If compelled by valid legal process, we disclose only what is legally required.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with data brokers, ad networks, or analytics resellers.

4. Advertising

The free tier of SheetSheep is supported by advertising, under a strict placement rule: ads appear only on the post-drill summary screen, after a drill has finished, or as static banners in menus.

  • No interstitial or audio advertising during an active practice session.
  • No ad may interrupt your rhythmic flow or hijack your device's audio session.
  • Microphone audio is never made available to any advertising SDK.
  • SheetSheep Pro removes all advertising.

5. Data retention

Locally stored practice data stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. If you use Google Sign-In, your profile and synced progress are retained while your account is active, and are deleted when you request deletion (see section 6). Purchase entitlement records are retained as required for accounting and tax compliance.

6. Your rights and account deletion

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your SheetSheep account and the associated personal data at any time by emailing sheetsheepsupport@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. Please use the subject line "Account deletion request".

We will action verified deletion requests within 30 days, removing your profile, synced progress records and associated identifiers from our systems. Data held only on your own device is removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. If you are in the EEA, the UK, California or another jurisdiction granting specific data subject rights, we honour those rights — including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection — through the same contact address.

7. Children's privacy

SheetSheep is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data in transit between the app and our backend is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Access to stored profile data is limited to what is necessary to operate the service. No system is perfectly secure, but the most sensitive thing you could give us — your piano's audio — never leaves your device in the first place.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date above and surface the change in the app or on this page. Continued use of SheetSheep after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
