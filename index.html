Built like a practice tool, not a game

Three things do the work. The third is the one nobody else does well.

On-device audio

Play your actual piano. It listens.

SheetSheep uses your device microphone to detect the pitch you play in real time. Acoustic upright or digital keyboard — no cables, no MIDI adapter, no screenshots of a waterfall. Just you, the page, and the key.

Audio is processed live on the device and is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. Read the microphone clause.

Reading

Treble & bass clef decoding

Landmark and directional reading — steps and skips anchored to reference notes — so you recognise intervals at a glance instead of counting up from the bottom line every time.

Drills

Tactile, real-time feedback

Chunky, pressable keys with immediate pitch validation and a deviation readout. Short sessions, honest scoring, and procedural measure generation so you can't memorise your way past the exercise.
